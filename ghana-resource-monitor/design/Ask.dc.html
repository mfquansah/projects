Example conversation. Numbers come from the early prototype model.
What does Ghana's energy forecast look like over the next couple of years?

If peak demand keeps growing about 7% a year, it rises from 4,283 MW in 2025 to roughly 4,900 MW in 2027. With capacity near the current estimate of 4,968 MW and no new plants, the reserve margin shrinks from 16% to about 1% by 2027, and demand overtakes capacity in 2028.

How answers are made

1
It reads the data. Every figure comes from the database, tagged with its source and year.
2
It runs the model. Forecasts and scenarios are computed by the forecast engine, never written from memory.
3
It explains. The answer states what is known, how sure we are, and where to check.
If the data does not cover a question, the assistant says so instead of guessing.
