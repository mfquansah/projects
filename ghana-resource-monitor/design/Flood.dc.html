Where Ghana floods, and why.

Satellite radar shows where water has stood after heavy rain. Rainfall records and land cover show what changed around it. Together they help separate a changing climate from the way we build and drain our cities.

Where the data comes from

Sentinel-1 radar (flood extent) CHIRPS and GPM (rainfall) Sentinel-2 and Landsat (built-up area) Elevation model (low ground) Ghana Meteorological Agency (stations) NADMO (event reports)

Why this module needs care

Separating climate change from poor drainage and planning is hard, and the answer differs by place. We will show the uncertainty and explain the method, the same way the energy module does.
