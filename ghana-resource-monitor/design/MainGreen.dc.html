What changes the picture

Gas supply
Most of Ghana's power comes from thermal plants. When gas runs short, available capacity falls below what the plants are rated for.
Hydro levels
Akosombo, Bui and Kpong depend on rainfall. Dry years cut output and limit when plants can run.
New capacity
Plants and solar projects add supply, but projects slip. The model tests on-time and delayed cases.
Cooling and heat
Hotter evenings push air-conditioning load into the peak hours.
Electric vehicles
A small load today. The tool shows how much it would take for charging to matter, and how timing changes that.
Transmission and payments
Thin lines in some corridors and unpaid bills in the sector can cause cuts even when plants are available.
