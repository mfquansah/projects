About

For the past 10 years I've worked across education, big tech and pharma, in teams in Ghana, the US and London. In every role I've done the same core thing: talk to users, understand what they need, and use data to build products and features that answer their questions. I'm most interested in how data can shape great products, from the first user conversation to the shipped feature.
