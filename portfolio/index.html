About me

I like solving problems, tinkering with ideas, and building things I wish existed.

My curiosity usually starts with a problem I've run into, a question I can't stop thinking about, or a gap I keep noticing.

My background is in data analytics and product management, and I like bringing the two together to turn ideas into useful products.

AI has made building easier than ever, so I'm experimenting with my own ideas more. I still care about doing it thoughtfully: understanding the problem, making deliberate decisions, and documenting enough of the journey to learn from it.

This site is where I share what I'm building, thinking about, and learning. Right now, that's Ghana Resource Monitor, a tool to track resource use in Ghana, where I'm from, and Dwene, a platform for learning causal inference.
